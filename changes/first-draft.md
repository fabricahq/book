The book is live and written in public. Most pages are still outlines; these are drafted so far.

- [Principles](../01-introduction/04-principles.md): the ideas that run through the whole book, starting with introspecting every agent session.
- [The hierarchy of bottlenecks](../01-introduction/05-the-hierarchy-of-bottlenecks.md): the order bottlenecks show up in, and why only the current one matters.
- [Attribution](../01-introduction/07-attribution.md): where the book's ideas come from, and how to flag one that needs credit.
- [The dev lifecycle](../03-the-dev-lifecycle/index.md): the stages everything you ship goes through, and which ones you can skip.
- [Shape](../03-the-dev-lifecycle/02-shape/index.md): working out what to build and how, with real software instead of sketches.
- [Design](../03-the-dev-lifecycle/02-shape/02-design.md): getting in sync with your agent by letting it interview you.
- [Deliver](../03-the-dev-lifecycle/03-deliver/index.md): taking each slice of work from plan to production.
- [Orchestration](../05-orchestration/index.md): running many agents at once, and choosing when to look at them.
- [Human attention is the bottleneck](../05-orchestration/02-human-attention.md): why your one focus token limits how many agents you can run.
