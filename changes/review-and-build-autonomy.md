title: Updated the hierarchy of bottlenecks with review and build autonomy
---

Once an agent can check its own work, the next thing to slow you down is reviewing whether it's what you wanted.

- [The hierarchy of bottlenecks](../01-introduction/05-the-hierarchy-of-bottlenecks.md): now has seven bottlenecks, in a new order: verification, review, build autonomy, integration, session capacity, cognitive load, and learning. Review splits off from verification, autonomy becomes build autonomy, and integration moves ahead of session capacity.
