Everything you ship goes through the same lifecycle. But depending on what you're shipping, you can skip some of its stages.

The lifecycle has three parts. A [spark](01-spark.md) makes you want to build or somehow change your software. When the work is new or uncertain, you [shape](02-shape/) it, working out what to build and how. Then you [deliver](03-deliver/) it, one slice at a time, from a plan to production.

A one-line bug fix might go straight from spark to build, validate, merge, and deploy. A brand-new product uses every stage. Maintenance work, such as dependency upgrades, refactors, and flaky tests, fits the same lifecycle. Part of the skill is knowing which stages a change needs.
